OnSIR: Ontology for Seed Irradiation and Plant Radiobiology

A FAIR OWL 2 DL ontology of seed-irradiation treatments and their dose-dependent biological effects, aligned to BFO, PO, NCBITaxon, ChEBI, PATO and ENVO with verified IRIs and to QUDT for units, with dose-to-effect axioms supporting DL reasoning.

Taxon-specific dose windows

Dose categories are not asserted: each taxon carries numeric windows, so a reasoner derives the category of a dose from its value together with the taxon. Bounds are literature-derived and provenanced per class.
