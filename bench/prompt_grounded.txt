Taxon-specific dose windows, as encoded in OWL 2 datatype facets. A dose at or below the optimum is at_or_below_optimum; above the optimum but below the LD50 is above_optimum; at or above the LD50 is at_or_above_ld50.

  Capsicum annuum: reported optimum 50 Gy, reported LD50 200 Gy
  Nicotiana tabacum: reported optimum 15 Gy, reported LD50 25 Gy
  Trigonella foenum-graecum: reported optimum 150 Gy, reported LD50 350 Gy
  Vigna unguiculata: reported optimum 80 Gy, reported LD50 132 Gy

These windows are evidence-relative: they record where a dose falls with respect to the values reported in the literature for that species, and carry no claim about the biological outcome.
